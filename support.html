Does audio keep playing when the screen is locked?

Yes. Sleep Sounds supports background audio. Lock your iPhone and playback continues. Use Control Center or the lock screen to pause/resume.

How do I mix sounds?

Tap any sound to start it, then tap more to layer them. Open the Mixer tab to set the volume of each layer individually, or pick a ready-made blend from Presets.

What does Pro unlock?

Pro unlocks every sound, the multi-track mixer and the sleep timer. It's available as a monthly or yearly subscription (each with a 7-day free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Open it from Settings → Upgrade to Pro.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel?

Restore: Settings → Restore Purchases. Cancel: iPhone Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions.

The sounds won't play

Make sure your device isn't in silent mode and the volume is up. If a sound still won't start, please email the address above and tell us your iPhone model and iOS version.
